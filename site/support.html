Support

PreApply is open source and maintained with the community. Here's how to get help or help others.

The questions look wrong or out of date

Click the pencil icon ("Questions changed?") in the PreApply panel. It opens a GitHub form already filled in with the accelerator. Or open the form directly.

An accelerator is missing

Add it here. Paste its questions one per line. Accelerators are only added together with their questions, copied from the real form.

Something in the extension doesn't work

Report a bug with the page address and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

The pill doesn't appear on an accelerator's site.
PreApply only shows on accelerators in its list. For accelerators added recently, open the PreApply toolbar popup and click "Enable" if it asks.

Does PreApply fill in applications for me?
No. It never reads or fills in forms. Use the copy button to take the questions to your own notes or AI agent.

Is my data collected?
No. See the privacy policy.
